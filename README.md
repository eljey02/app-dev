# Titanic (1997)

My favorite movie is **Titanic**, directed by *James Cameron*. It's a classic romance and disaster film set on the real-life sinking of the RMS Titanic in 1912. 

> "You jump, I jump, right?"

---

## Main Characters
* **Jack Dawson** - played by Leonardo DiCaprio
* **Rose DeWitt Bukater** - played by Kate Winslet
* **Cal Hockley** - played by Billy Zane

| Info | Details |

## Why I like it
1. The cinematography and the scale of the ship look crazy good even today.
2. The soundtrack (*My Heart Will Go On*) is iconic.
3. The contrast between the first-class passengers and third-class passengers makes the story hit harder.

### Random Fact
The scenes set in `1912` actually last about **2 hours and 40 minutes**, which is the exact time it took for the real ship to sink.
